What Xomtra adds to Home Assistant

Home Assistant shows you the present. Xomtra turns its history into answers.

years

Years of history

States and statistics kept for months or years in compressed Parquet files, without loading the Home Assistant database.

daily

Findings every day

Entity catalog, data quality checks, correlations between devices and anomalies, ready before you ask.

read-only

Safe by design

AI can read, never switch anything. Roles for family members, and every query is logged.

Questions

Do I need Claude?

No. Xomtra works with Claude Desktop, Claude.ai and any client that speaks MCP. Your AI subscription is your own; Xomtra never pays or sees it.

Does my data leave the house?

Only the answers your AI client asks for, under your account. Xomtra itself sends nothing anywhere unless you turn on anonymous install counting.

How much disk does it need?

A typical home needs 2–5 GB for 90 days of states and 5 years of hourly statistics. Large setups with years of states should use the Proxmox or VMware appliance.

Is it affiliated with Home Assistant?

No. Xomtra is an independent open source project built for Home Assistant.
